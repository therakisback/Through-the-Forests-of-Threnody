# Through the Forests of Threnody
### A small game demo inspired by the Brandon Sanderson Novella 'Shadows for Silence in the Forests of Hell'

## Overview
This project was created as a project for University College Dublin's Game Development class. 
With the requirement of making a "corridor game", and inspired by the popular mobile game "Into the Dead". 
Through the Forests of Threnody is a game about being a courier in a world that has three Simple Rules:
 * Do not kindle a flame
 * Do not shed the blood of another
 * Do not run during the night
This game comes from imagining what such a world would be like, and how people would handle emergencies, or important deliveries.

## Features
* Complete runs to earn money, with a slowly rising difficulty level.
* Duck between trees in procedurally generated forests.
* Run from the hand-modelled and rigged Shades as they spot you and chase you down.
* Use money to acquire upgrades and tools to increase your chance of survival on the runs.
* Use tools to distract or fight against shades, but be careful not to overuse them!
* Play on mobile or desktop.
